# Codebase Audit, Bug Fixes & Zero-Quality-Loss Optimizations (2026-11-22)

This document summarizes the full codebase audit, bug fixes, zero-quality-loss optimizations, and structural cleanups landed on `main` (`dino-yatzy-mosi`). The in-progress Chapter 6 branch (`dino-kafli6-gramedla`) was intentionally left untouched and audited separately.

## 2. Bug Fixes by Subsystem

### A. Core Engine, Camera, Lifecycle & `main.js`
- **Spoken-line 0 s skip on blocked autoplay (`remake/engine/scene/scene.js`):** When `audio.play()` rejects (`voice.done(sound) && !voice.started(sound)`), `sayTask` now falls back to reading-pace duration (`lineDuration(entry)`) instead of completing immediately. `stop()` also clears actor `control(null)`, `setOverlay(null)`, and footstep dust.
- **Voice pause/resume & Blob URL leaks (`remake/engine/scene/voice.js`):** Fixed `setPaused(false)` closure over mutable `current`, guarded `play()` while paused, cleared `older.src = ''` when replacing an element, and added `dispose()` to revoke preloaded Blob URLs.
- **Caption clearing (`remake/engine/scene/ui.js`):** `clearCaption()` called with no argument now clears the active caption instead of matching `undefined`.
- **Runaway FOV after flight camera stop (`remake/engine/camera/flight-camera.js`, `director.js`):** `flightCamera.stop()` stops the director, zeroes `state.fast`, and short-circuits `step()`. Added division-by-zero guards for `duration <= 0`, `fovSeconds <= 0`, `standAlong`, `herd`, `floatTo`/`floatDown` (`riding.js`), and `slideDown` (`neck-ride.js`).
- **Shadow VRAM leak & context restore (`remake/engine/shadows.js`):** Restores `target.textures[0]` to the unused RGBA8 colour texture on `dispose()` so Three.js deallocates it, and rebuilds FBO attachments cleanly on `webglcontextrestored` without binding `DepthTexture` to `COLOR_ATTACHMENT0`.
- **Cross-scene fog shader clobbering (`remake/engine/fog.js`, `warm-up.js`):** Stored per-scene `fogParsFragment` in a `WeakMap` and hooked `scene.onBeforeRender` + `applyFog(scene)` so multi-scene rendering (e.g. Háls og hali over a chapter) never leaves the wrong look's fog constants active.
- **Character LOD worker & lifecycle races (`remake/engine/character-lod.js`, `simplify.js`, `eyelids.js`, `flier-saddle.js`):** Fixed dead worker on `onerror`, added `!job` guard, transferred all 4–5 buffers, added 5 s idle worker termination, keyed `levelsOf` by level config + evicted rejected promises, added `released` `WeakSet` race guard against in-flight `prepareCharacter()`, normalized `-0.0` to `+0.0` before bit-hashing in `simplify.js`, and synced off-screen `followsBody` visibility for eyelids and saddles.
- **Actor gait probing & disposal (`remake/engine/actor.js`):** Snapshotted and restored all bone & model transforms around `measureGait` in `gaitOf()` so walk measurement never bakes overlay/footing offsets or disturbs live planted feet; evicted rejected `loadTemplate` promises; guarded `NaN` look limits; added full `dispose()`.
- **Cross-tab / iframe save overwrites (`remake/engine/save.js`):** Detects external storage changes via `lastRaw` and merges on write so simultaneous writes from the main page and embedded mini-game iframes never clobber each other, while safely staying in memory when storage is read-only or quota-full.
- **Location release race & other engine guards (`remake/engine/locations.js`, `gler.js`, `plate-glow.js`, `skin-surface.js`, `shell.js`, `renderer.js`, `scheduler.js`, `map-moment-scene.js`, `journal.js`):** Added generation counter to `locations.js` disposing orphaned mid-prepare builds; validated `gler.js` settings, clamped >= 1 px targets, wrapped render target state in `try/finally`, and added `dispose()`; stopped `plate-glow.js` `update()` after `dispose()`; replaced wrap-prone integer spatial hash in `skin-surface.js` with string keys; guarded empty capsules/antiparallel lean in `shell.js`; guarded `renderer.js` resize while canvas is lent to Háls og hali; fixed duplicate journal flight DOM leak and runaway scheduler error surfacing.
- **Main loop & pause coordination (`remake/main.js`):** Added ref-counted `clockHolds` (mirroring `audio/hold.js`) across book, Villa, and mini-games; guarded `loading` hide timeouts with `starShows`; fixed `visibilitychange` `AudioContext` resume; skipped `stats`/`adapt` on >0.5 s tab-backgrounding gaps; fixed `minnisspil` `closeType: 'memory:close'`.

### B. World & Rendering (`remake/world/**` — 29 files)
- **Crowd LOD corruption & material ownership (`critters/crowd.js`, `forest/life.js`, `critters/crowd-models.js`):** Gave every LOD level its own `flight` `InstancedBufferAttribute` (fixing `copyWithin` cross-LOD corruption), tracked `ownMaterial` to prevent disposing shared cached GLTF materials or double-disposing in `life.js`, fixed `'mushroom'` -> `'mushrooms'` typo, and fixed frog return-hop asymmetry.
- **Async disposal & camera-far leaks (`props/far-friends.js`, `ardalur/far-land.js`, `ardalur/sections.js`, `ardalur/index.js`, `forest/butterflies.js`, `props/mud-prints.js`):** Added `disposed` checks after every `await` in `far-friends.js` and properly disposed prefetch-parsed meshes/eyelids/sparkles; restored `camera.far` on `far-land.js` `dispose()`; evicted rejected hero butterfly promises and disposed tinted clones; replaced permanent module-level `digitGeometries` cache in `mud-prints.js` with instance-scoped disposal; added `queue.clear()` and `try/finally` disposal in Árdalur.
- **Additive fog glow bleed (`forest/atmosphere.js`, `props/mist.js`, `props/glow-flower.js`, `props/sparkles.js`):** Replaced `#include <fog_fragment>` on `AdditiveBlending` shaders with `gl_FragColor.rgb *= (1.0 - fogFactor)` so distant additive particles fade out instead of adding bright fog colour.
- **Seamless Árdalur tree belt & stable LOD culling (`ardalur/belt.js`, `ardalur/index.js`, `forest/forest.js`, `forest/batch.js`):** Replaced sequential section RNG with pure cell-coordinate hashing in `belt.js` (eliminating section-boundary tree seams); reused stable batch arrays so `cullBatches()` `WeakMap` camera-cut hysteresis works again; added camera move/turn culling throttle and `cullSigOf()` staleness detection.
- **Shader & geometry optimizations (`forest/grass.js`, `forest/terrain.js`, `forest/materials.js`, `props/water.js`, `props/waterfall.js`, `ardalur/look.js`, `ardalur/river.js`, `ardalur/banks.js`, `forest/plan.js`, `props/basket.js`, `props/footprints.js`):** Hoisted grass per-blade colour/dapple/actor-shade to the vertex shader and sorted visible cells near-to-far; fused terrain actor-shade passes and shared `noiseTexture` into water/mist; added `PRINT_WATER` early discard & `RAINBOW_FRAG` early-out; replaced `pow()` chains with squaring; reduced Voronoi leaf texture baking to 3x3 (bit-identical across tested seeds); replaced 0.25 m step-marching in `banks.js:inView` with O(1) analytical segment-cylinder distance; fixed `thinDepth` `DoubleSide` shadow caster material matching; hoisted `dFdx`/`dFdy` + `textureGrad` in `ardalur/look.js`; guarded `basket.js` bounds and `footprints.js` empty updates.

## 4. Intentionally Preserved Contracts

Several audit suggestions were deliberately adjusted or skipped to preserve verified engine and test invariants:

- **`THREE.RGBADepthPacking` in `forest/materials.js`:** Retained because Three.js r180 `WebGLShadowMap` renders directional shadows into an RGBA `WebGLRenderTarget` expecting RGBA depth packing.
- **`font-display: block` in `style.css` & `book.css`:** Retained (`swap` applied to `spil.css` & `sloedla.css`) per `remake/tests/fonts.test.mjs`.
- **`aEdge` / `aPrint` attributes in `props/mud-prints.js` & `forestLeafShadow` in `forest/grass.js`:** Retained per `fen.test.mjs` and `forest-glade.test.mjs` shader contracts.
- **`net.js` `MISSED_PINGS` threshold:** Retained per `fjolspil-net.test.mjs` presence timing assertions.
- **`map-page.js` WebGL context retention & `map-life.js` `requestIdleCallback` hook:** Retained per `book.test.mjs` lifecycle contracts (eager `setTimeout(..., 0)` fallback removed).
- **`hals.prewarm()` on `Leikir` tab open (`main.js`):** Retained via lazy `hals` proxy (so page startup skips loading Háls og hali while `kafli2.test.mjs` tab-open prewarm contract remains intact).
