# Veiddu (Uggeðla's Go Fish) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.
> Owner's process for this game: one builder agent per task (Sonnet where the work is clear), no reviewer agents; the controller checks each task (tests, captures, a read of the diff) and shows the owner a video and stills after each chunk.

**Goal:** A Go Fish card game for a 6-year-old (Egill): the child collects pairs of Fen animals against Mosi and Uggeðla, opened from the book's Leikir page by Uggeðla's star, with Fjölspil last.

**Architecture:** A new game beside "Eitt!" in `remake/engine/veiddu/`: pure rules and minds (no DOM), a DOM table in Eitt!'s look and pace, and Uggeðla live in a three.js window like Kambeðla's. Shared pieces are imported from Eitt! and the book, never copied or changed: `book/fx.js`, `audio/game-music.js`, `scene/voice.js`, `eitt/skill.js`, `actor.js`, `fjolspil/seats.js`.

**Tech Stack:** Plain ES modules in the browser, three.js (import map), Node's test runner, ffmpeg for images, ElevenLabs v4 via `remake/tools/voice-lines.mjs`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-31-veiddu-design.md` (Icelandic). Read it first; this plan does not repeat its reasons.

## Review Focus

1. **Taps while something moves.** A double tap on a card or on the pond, or a tap during a flight or an AI turn, must do nothing. The rules throw on an illegal act, and the table ignores input while busy. Pinned in Task 1 (rules throw) and Task 3 (`phase` is `busy`, the lab check taps twice).
2. **Empty hands and an empty pond.** A seat that runs out of cards, a seat whose turn comes with no cards and no pond, and the last pairs asked for after the pond is empty must never deadlock. Pinned in Task 1 (1,000 seeded games all end with 26 pairs within 400 acts).
3. **Ties.** Two or three seats with the most pairs all win, and the child in a tie gets the sticker. Pinned in Task 1 (`winners`) and Task 8 (sticker on a tie).
4. **Illegal AI asks.** A mind never asks a seat with no cards, never asks for a kind it does not hold, and never asks for a kind whose other copies are all in pairs already. Pinned in Task 1.
5. **Small and upright screens.** A hand of 13 cards at 820×1180 and on a phone held sideways (844×390) shows every card at 88 px or more, in rows, with nothing under the windows or the pond. Pinned in Task 2 (captures at three sizes) and Task 3 (the lab check measures card widths).
