# Critters: where they come from

- `smaedla.glb`, the small lizard (smáeðla) of chapter 4's flight: the owner's own model, made by him in Tripo3D
  (`smaedla-tripo-v1.glb`, 2026-11-05), with his shape and paint. `remake/tools/prep-crowd.mjs` made it lighter and moving
  (a near and a far level, its paint baked to 512 px, a skeleton from `remake/tools/crowd-rigs/smaedla.json` and the clips
  run, idle, drink and look frozen into morph targets); nothing in it was drawn or modelled by anyone else.
- `grasbitur.glb`, the grazer (grasbítur) of chapter 4's flight: the owner's own model, made by him in Tripo3D
  (`grasbitur-tripo-v1.glb`, 2026-11-06), with his shape and paint. `remake/tools/prep-crowd.mjs` made it lighter and moving
  (a near and a far level, its paint baked to 512 px, a skeleton from `remake/tools/crowd-rigs/grasbitur.json` and the clips
  walk, trot, graze and look frozen into morph targets); nothing in it was drawn or modelled by anyone else.
- `flugdyr.glb`, the flyer (flugdýr) of chapter 4's flight: the owner's own model, made by him in Tripo3D
  (`flugdyr-tripo-v1.glb`, 2026-11-06), with his shape and paint. `remake/tools/prep-crowd.mjs` made it lighter and moving
  (a near and a far level, its paint baked to 512 px, a skeleton from `remake/tools/crowd-rigs/flugdyr.json` that lays his
  hanging wings flat in flight, and the clips flap, glide and perch frozen into morph targets); nothing in it was drawn or
  modelled by anyone else.
- `butterfly.glb`: see `butterfly-source.json`.
